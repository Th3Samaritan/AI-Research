TRAINING COMPUTE-OPTIMAL LARGE LANGUAGE MODELS · the paper that shrank the models

Same compute.
4× smaller. 4× more data. Better.

In 2020, Kaplan's scaling laws said: given more compute, grow the model (N ∝ C0.73). Everyone obeyed — GPT-3, Gopher, MT-NLG. In 2022 DeepMind re-ran the experiment with one methodological fix and found the advice was wrong: model and data should grow in equal proportion (N ∝ C0.50), roughly 20 tokens per parameter. The proof: Chinchilla — 70B parameters on 1.4T tokens, same compute as the 280B Gopher — beat it everywhere.
