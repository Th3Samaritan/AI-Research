ZERO → MASTERY · the layer everybody skips

The model never
reads your text.

It reads integers — and a separate, frozen, dumb little program decided what those integers are before the model existed. Nearly every strange thing an LLM does traces back to that program. Here you'll build one from raw bytes upward: train real merges on a real corpus, watch the sequence shrink, encode and decode, then reproduce every famous tokenizer footgun on purpose.
