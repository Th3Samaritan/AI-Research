LLAMA: OPEN AND EFFICIENT FOUNDATION LANGUAGE MODELS · the paper that launched the open-model era

Smaller model.
More tokens.

Chinchilla said scale the model. LLaMA said scale the data. A 13B model trained on 1 trillion public tokens outperforms GPT-3 at 175B — not because it saw smarter data, but because it saw enough of it. Three architectural tweaks and the courage to train past the "optimal" point made LLaMA the model that opened everything.
